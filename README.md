# 🎮 GAME ARENA

## live URL of GameArena:- https://kabirvyas-gamearena.netlify.app


### Created by KABIR VYAS

**GAME ARENA** is an interactive web-based gaming hub featuring three classic mini-games built with modern web technologies, custom sound effects, victory celebrations, and instant score tracking.

---

## 🕹️ The Games

### 1. ✊ Rock-Paper-Scissors (Best of 5)
* Classic Best of 5 match against a smart bot.
* First player to win 3 rounds takes the match.
* Live round history feed and animated choice reveal.

### 2. ❌ Tic-Tac-Toe
* **vs Bot Mode**: Single-player vs an AI that blocks and takes smart moves.
* **Duel Mode**: 2-player pass-and-play on the same device.
* Animated winning line when 3 in a row is achieved.

### 3. 🧩 6x6 Sudoku
* **New puzzle every time**: Procedural algorithm generates unique puzzles every game.
* **Two Difficulty Levels**:
  * **Beginner**: Plenty of starter clues for quick solving.
  * **Pro**: Fewer starter clues for a challenge.
* **Tools**: Live timer, mistake counter (max 3), Pencil notes mode, and Hints.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Modern Dark Theme**: Sleek obsidian and sapphire design with glassmorphism.
* **Sound Effects**: Custom audio for moves, victory fanfare, and alerts with a mute button.
* **Victory Confetti**: Celebratory particle effects when you win.
* **Automatic Saving**: Game records, win rates, and fastest times are saved in browser storage.
* **Export Stats**: Download your match history anytime as a CSV or JSON file.
* **Player Profiles**: Choose your custom name and avatar emoji.
